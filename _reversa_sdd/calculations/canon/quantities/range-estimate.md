# Range estimate at the minimum-drag speed · `R_est`

Distance on one battery, flying at the minimum-drag speed `V_md` of the airplane. An estimate, because the speed of least battery energy per metre can lie elsewhere once the drive train is part of the balance; within the domain of [[max-range]] it never exceeds it (#1235).
