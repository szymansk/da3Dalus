# Spanwise bending moment of the main wing · `M(y)`

Bending moment along the main wing, referred to the centreline at the root row, for the right
half (`Y > 0`; the load is symmetric). Aerodynamic load only: no inertial relief — conservative for positive g, not for
negative g or heavy tip masses (see [[spanwise-loads]]).

**Unit.** `N*m`

**Produced by.** [[spanwise-loads]]
