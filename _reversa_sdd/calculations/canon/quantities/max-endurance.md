# Greatest endurance · `t_max`

Longest flight time on one battery: the energy over the least battery power, `t_max = 3600·E_bat / min P_bat(V)` over the speeds the airplane can fly at full throttle or less. The optimised value; the value at the airplane's own minimum-sink speed is [[endurance-estimate]] (#1235). A property of the aircraft.
