# Battery power in level flight · `P_bat`

Electrical power the battery delivers when the airplane flies level at the given speed with the thrust equal to the drag. Route B (published `R_m`): motor terminal voltage times motor current. Route A: propeller shaft power from the APC table over the motor efficiency. Replaces the quotient 'aerodynamic power over a total efficiency' (#1235).

**Unit.** `W`
