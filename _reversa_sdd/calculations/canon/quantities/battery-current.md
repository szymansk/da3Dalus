# Battery current in level flight · `I_bat`

Current the battery delivers when the airplane flies level at the given speed with the thrust equal to the drag: `I_bat = P_bat / U_bat`. To be compared with [[battery-current-allowed]] by the evaluation; the canon states both as values, never a verdict (A10).
