# Greatest range · `R_max`

Longest distance on one battery: the energy over the least battery energy per metre, `R_max = 3600·E_bat / min (P_bat(V)/V)` over the speeds the airplane can fly at full throttle or less. The optimised value; the value at the airplane's own minimum-drag speed is [[range-estimate]] (#1235). A property of the aircraft.

**Unit.** `m`
