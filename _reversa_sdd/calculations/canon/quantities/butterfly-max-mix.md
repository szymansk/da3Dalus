# Largest crow mix at the approach speed · `s_max`

Largest crow mix that can be added at the approach speed flown without crow before the stall speed reaches it; absent if the stall speed does not rise with the mix. The mix is a fraction of the given full-crow angles (`s = 1`: [[crow-flap-deflection]], [[crow-aileron-deflection]]), not of a stop.

**Unit.** `dimensionless`

**Produced by.** [[butterfly-approach]]
