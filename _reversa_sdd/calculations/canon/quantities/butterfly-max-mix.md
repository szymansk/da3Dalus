# Largest crow mix at the approach speed · `s_max`

Largest crow mix that can be added at the approach speed flown without crow before the stall speed reaches it; absent if the stall speed does not rise with the mix. The mix runs from the landing configuration (`s = 0`) to the given full-crow angles (`s = 1`: [[crow-flap-deflection]], [[crow-aileron-deflection]]), not to a stop.

**Unit.** `1`
