# Endurance estimate at the minimum-sink speed · `t_est`

Flight time on one battery, flying at the minimum-sink speed `V_mp` of the airplane (the speed of least aerodynamic power). An estimate, because the speed of least battery power can lie elsewhere once the drive train is part of the balance; within the domain of [[max-endurance]] it never exceeds it (#1235).

**Unit.** `s`
