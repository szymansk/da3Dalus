# Thrust demand over full-throttle thrust · `r_T`

Drag at the flight speed divided by the thrust the fitted motor and propeller deliver there at full throttle ([[thrust-at-airspeed]]). A value above 1 says the required thrust exceeds what full throttle gives: the speed cannot be held in level flight and the part-load values at that speed describe a demand, not a state. A value, not a verdict (A10).

**Unit.** `1`

**Produced by.** [[motor-propeller-part-load]]
