# Load factor · `n`

Ratio of lift to weight. An **input of the lift balance**, bound per application: 1 in straight level flight, `n_lim` for the envelope. In the sustained turn it is an optimisation variable, not this input. Until 2026-10-01 it was produced from a bank angle (`turn-load-factor`, deleted).

**Unit.** `dimensionless`
