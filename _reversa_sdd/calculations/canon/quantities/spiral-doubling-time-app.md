# Spiral time to double on approach · `T_2,spiral,app`

Time in which the bank angle of a spirally unstable aircraft doubles, `ln 2 / λ_s`. For a stable spiral
the bank halves instead; the entry reports that time with the sign marked. It is what Sadraey limits
(§12.3.3, Tab. 12.15), not the sign of the spiral criterion.

**Unit.** `s`

**Produced by.** [[lateral-static-stability-app]]
