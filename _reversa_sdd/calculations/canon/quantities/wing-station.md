# Spanwise station · `y_st`

The distance from the centreline along the half span of the main wing, measured in the wing's y-z plane. It is the abscissa of [[spanwise-lift-distribution]], [[spanwise-shear-force]] and [[spanwise-bending-moment]] and nothing else: the caller supplies no value, the formula chooses the stations. Listed so that a curve says what its `x` is.

**Unit.** `m`
