# Stall break angle · `alpha_break`

Angle of attack past the stall angle at which the lift coefficient of the computed polar has fallen to the fraction (1 - f) of C_L,max (f, stall-break-fraction). A final result: an angle, not an index into a sweep, and no classification of the stall (A10). Replaces stall-onset-index (#1219).

**Unit.** `deg`

**Produced by.** [[stall-break]]
