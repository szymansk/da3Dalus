> Generated by the Reversa Archaeologist.

> Later clusters append their own sections below.

## Conventions

* **Persistence:** SQLAlchemy 2.x ORM, SQLite by default (WAL), PostgreSQL
  supported through `SQLALCHEMY_DATABASE_URL`. Migrations via Alembic.
* **Implicit primary key:** every model inherits `app/db/base.py::Base`, which
  declares `id = Column(Integer, primary_key=True, index=True)` and derives
  `__tablename__` from the lower-cased class name unless the model overrides it.
  The `id` column is therefore **not repeated** in the tables below unless the
  model redeclares it. 🟢 CONFIRMED (`app/db/base.py:5-11`)
* **Units.** Two unit worlds coexist and the boundary is the converter layer:
  | Context | Length unit |
  |---|---|
  | DB geometry (`wing_xsecs.xyz_le`, `chord`, `fuselage_xsecs.a/b`, `weight_items.x_m`) | **metres** |
  | `wing_xsec_spares` dimensional fields (gh-402) | **millimetres** |
  | `WingConfig` / `cad_designer` topology | **millimetres** |
  | AeroSandbox / AVL | **metres** |
  Converters scale with `0.001` (mm→m) and `1000.0` (m→mm).
  🟢 CONFIRMED (`CLAUDE.md`, `app/converters/spare_origin_preservation.py:21-23`)
* **Angles** are degrees everywhere in the persisted model.
* **Transactions** are owned by the `get_db()` dependency (`app/db/session.py`);
  services never open their own transaction.

# Cluster D — mass, powertrain & versioning

Unit reminders for this cluster:

* **Mass** is stored in **kg** on `weight_items` and `aeroplanes.total_mass_kg`,
  but in **grams** on `components.mass_g`, `propeller_polars.weight_g` and
  `component_tree.weight_override_g`. The component-tree roll-up divides by
  1000 exactly once (`get_aircraft_total_weight_kg`).
* **Positions** on `weight_items` are **metres** in aeroplane coordinates.
* **Propeller geometry** is in **inches** (`diameter_in`, `pitch_in`) — the APC
  source unit; the performance service converts with `× 0.0254` at the call
  boundary. Every other propeller quantity is SI.
* **Versioning ids** are integer PKs, not UUIDs — the whole `/lineages`,
  `/branches` surface is integer-addressed while the rest of v2 uses UUIDs.
